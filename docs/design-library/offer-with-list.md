# Recipe: offer post with a list (square / 4:5)

Written in our own words from studying agency-made offer posts shared by Shoaib (2026-11-07). We take the principles, never the pixels: no artwork, text or logos are copied. Reference images stay private; this recipe is what the engine uses (`lib/layered/pro.ts`).

## Hierarchy (one reading path, three levels)
1. Kicker: small, capitals, wide letter-spacing.
2. Headline: one very heavy line, capitals, the biggest thing on the page.
3. Highlight bar: the 2-3 key words on a solid accent bar, rounded corners, soft shadow.
Then a list, then the contact bar. The eye goes top-left to bottom-right.

## Colour
- 2-3 colours only. One "hot" accent used on the highlight bar (and CTA), nowhere else.
- Everything else calm: light scrim behind the text, dark contact bar.
- Brand rule still wins: Citrus/Cobalt never carry text; text stays Ink or white.

## Shapes
- Rounded everything: logo pill, bar, list panel (radius about 3% of width).
- Photo subject sits right, large, allowed to feel close; text keeps clear of it.
- Soft shadow under the pill, bar and panel to lift them.

## List
- Max 4 points. Round icon (arrow) per point. One font size for all.
- Each point: bold key words, then light words, on the same line, wrapping to two lines at most.

## Contact bar
- Dark, edge to edge, about 11% of the height, same place on every design in a series.
- Round outlined icons; phone left, website right, large and bold.

## Series
- Same template, new photo and words, makes a campaign look designed. Keep the bar, pill and panel in the same places.

## Checks
- Spelling is ours (text is drawn in real fonts); contact details from the official source only.
- Subject must not sit behind the text column; background needs calm space on the left 60%.
